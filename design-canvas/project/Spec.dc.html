Building it with framework views

Theme. Theme.Material.Light, colorPrimary = surface (a white bar), windowLightStatusBar = true; values-night: Theme.Material.

Settings. A gear in the action bar (onCreateOptionsMenu) opens SettingsActivity; Home keeps only status and the three rows.

Cards. LinearLayout with @drawable/card (shape, 16dp corners, 1dp stroke). Rows inside use ?android:selectableItemBackground.

Switches. android.widget.Switch with thumbTint and trackTint colour state lists from primary and divider.

Fields. EditText and Spinner backgrounds: an outlined shape with 12dp corners and a 1dp divider stroke (primary when focused).

Messages. A Toast now; a bottom bar like the one on Emails is a TextView in a FrameLayout, hidden after 2.5 s.
